FAQ

Questions, answered.

Clear expectations matter when you're building a digital business.

What is Discord Resellers?+

Discord Resellers is a learning, resource and community platform designed around legitimate Discord-related digital products, services, automation, websites and community resources.

Is the Free tier actually free?+

Yes. The front-end presents the Free tier at $0. Any real account or payment flow would need to be connected to a backend before launch.

What do I get with Pro?+

Pro is presented as access to advanced guides, research, templates, supplier resources and the Pro Resource Vault. Actual availability should match your published membership terms.

What is the Supplier Vault?+

It is a research area intended to organize supplier information and opportunities. “Verified” should mean your own documented review process, not a guarantee of quality, availability or compliance.

Can I become a seller?+

Yes, subject to marketplace eligibility, community rules and moderation. Sellers are responsible for their listings and customer relationships.

What can I sell?+

Eligible digital products and services such as templates, designs, websites, bots and automation resources. You must have the right to sell what you list and follow applicable platform/provider rules.

How do Seller Slots work?+

Seller Slots are promotional placements with the benefits shown on this page. Promotions remain subject to community rules, moderation and any applicable platform limits.

Do you guarantee supplier results?+

No. Supplier information is research, not a guarantee of sales, availability, profit or performance. Users are responsible for their own due diligence and compliance with relevant platform/provider terms.
